## 1. U.S. jets strike Iran's Qeshm Island (Middle East / Hormuz)
- U.S. fighter jets hit an IRGC missile launch site on Qeshm Island in the
  Strait of Hormuz, in retaliation for an IRGC drone attack on a U.S. radar
  at Sheikh Isa base, Bahrain. Iran reported no damage to residential areas
  or infrastructure and no casualties. The same night an ADNOC vessel was
  attacked in the Strait; 11 crew were hurt and 1 was missing.
- The vessel attack is reported as an attack by an unidentified projectile;
  it is NOT described as a mine strike and no attacker is named for it.

## 2. Ukraine destroys Crimea Bridge road span (Ukraine / Europe)
- Ukrainian drone and missile strikes brought down the main road arch of the
  19 km Crimea Bridge into the Kerch Strait, the second day running; the
  Russian-installed governor of Crimea reported 2 killed and traffic halted.
  Russia struck Kyiv's North and South bridges in reply.

## 3. Gaza winter rains flood tents, babies at risk (Gaza / Middle East)

  waiting to enter Gaza, and IOM is $173 million short for its winter
  shelter programme.

## 4. Russian jets breach Japan airspace (Japan / Asia)
- Two suspected Russian aircraft entered Japanese airspace off Okinoshima,
  Fukuoka, 04:07-04:12 on 17 November, about 3 minutes each — the first
  breach since September 2024. Japan scrambled fighter jets and lodged a
  very strong protest with Moscow. The aircraft are described as suspected
  Russian, not confirmed.
- https://rixnews.com/en/news/japan-scrambles-jets-after-suspected-russian-airspace-violation

## 5. U.S. loses measles-free status after 26 years (United States / Health)
- The Pan American Health Organization announced on 17 November that the
  United States had lost its measles elimination status after 26 years,
  following more than 12 months of uninterrupted transmission, with 5,412
  confirmed cases in 2026 as of 12 November — the most in 35 years.
